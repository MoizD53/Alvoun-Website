BEFORE ALVOUN

نفح ریز مشروبات

Naphareese Maschroubat

Naphareese Maschroubat (نفح ریز مشروبات, meaning 'fragrance-releasing beverages') is a name that suggests more than a drink. It alludes to breath, essence, and the quiet unfolding of purity into the everyday. From its earliest vision, the purpose was never display but distillation. What emerged was not a collection of products but a line of offerings shaped by restraint, each one carrying the trace of something considered, something quiet.

Alvoun followed as an extension of that same current. Devoted to mineral and alkaline water, it did not break away but flowed forward, drawing from the same still source. Its clarity is not just a feature but a principle. What it offers is not novelty but balance, not noise but depth, shaped by patience and guided by the same hand that defined its origin.

THE NAME

The name Alvoun draws from the Arabic word 'Alvan', meaning colour.

Though water is colourless and transparent, the language of water is expressed through the spectrum surrounding its pH — a visual vocabulary of acidity, neutrality and alkalinity. Alvoun takes this relationship between water and colour and makes it part of its identity.

UNDERSTANDING ALKALINE WATER

The term 'alkaline' in alkaline water refers to its pH level, a measure of acidity or alkalinity on a scale from 0 to 14. Alvoun's multistage process enhances electrolytes and infuses minerals such as Calcium, Magnesium, and Potassium.

Potential benefits discussed in relation to alkaline water include ultra-hydration, mineral contribution, and acid neutralization.
